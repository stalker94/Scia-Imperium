# Scia-Imperium

Different from its brothers from the Drakabuh, the Scia Empire always stood out for technological advancement, a social welfare assured among its own and a way of life far from pursuing the knowledge of the Gods but rather based on research, science and atheist development as a civilization of a kind nature with its allies and cautious with its enemies.
TRAITS:
